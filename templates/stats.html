Rating Formula

Rating = 50 + created matches x4 + joined matches x3 + screenings hosted x4 + spots filled. Capped at 99.

XP = created matches x20 + joined matches x10 + screenings hosted x20.

Level = every 100 XP increases your level.

PAC rewards joining and attending.

SHO rewards creating matches and filling spots.

PAS reflects playmaking through hosted activity.

DRI rewards active participation.

DEF rewards reliability and filled squads.

PHY rewards overall activity volume.
